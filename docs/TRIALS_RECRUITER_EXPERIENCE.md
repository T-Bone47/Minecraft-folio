## 1. The Recruiter Fast-Track Journey

Recruiters evaluating software and engineering candidates often have limited time (< 3 minutes). The **Frontier Engineering Trials** accommodates this through three purpose-built rapid pathways:

### Pathway A: Operations Terminal Fast-Track Demo
1. Enter Engineering HQ at `[-26, 0, -22]` or press `[E]` on the **Operations Console**.
2. Click **"WATCH FAST-TRACK DEMO"**.
3. Opens Step 7 of the Recruiter Walkthrough Modal, detailing how "The Missing Four Tenths" incident was solved with verified telemetry traces in $< 60$ seconds.

### Pathway B: Direct In-World NPC Conversation
1. Approach any of the three Lead Engineers stationed outside their facility:
   - **Marcus Vance** (`[-10, 0, -5]`, Garage): Race Performance.
   - **Dr. Aris Thorne** (`[2, 0, 21]`, AI Lab): Machine Learning.
   - **Viktor Cole** (`[12, 0, -14]`, Telemetry Tower): Distributed Systems.
2. Press `[E]` to converse.
3. Dialogue presents the operational incident in plain technical English.
4. Click "BEGIN TRIAL WORKBENCH" to launch the scenario directly.

### Pathway C: Exportable Evidence Report (JSON)
- Upon completing or viewing any trial, recruiters can click **"EXPORT DEBRIEF (JSON)"**.
- Generates a standalone, machine-readable debrief file containing the candidate's diagnostic steps, rubric score, causal explanations, and source file citations.
