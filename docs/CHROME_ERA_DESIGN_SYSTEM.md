# Chrome Era design system

Identity: ASISH — THE ENGINEERING FRONTIER / CHROME ERA ENGINEERING CAMPUS. Colour tokens live in `data/chromeEra.ts`; UI counterparts use CSS variables. Silver #C8CDD2 and bright silver #E8EBED are architectural surfaces; graphite #292D34 and carbon #171A20 are structure/equipment; racing red #D71D35 and deep red #A51125 are focal details. Warm white #F4F3EF and metallic grey #7E8790 balance the palette. Green/amber/blue remain semantic data/status colours.

Original industrial pixel patterns use 16×16 nearest-filter textures. Material profiles distinguish polished, brushed, graphite, red paint, carbon, matte tiled floor, white lighting and technical glass. They do not recolour grass, trees, football pitches or model-output semantics.

Historical research: official MP4-23 and Hamilton heritage pages informed the broad silver/red period palette, not exact geometry, sponsor art or performance claims. A modernised playable variant retains its current cockpit protection frame and digital instruments, with a label explaining that it is not period-exact.
