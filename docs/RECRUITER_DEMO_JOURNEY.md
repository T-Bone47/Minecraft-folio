## 1. Fast-Track Rationale & UX Design

Technical recruiters and engineering hiring managers often have less than 2 minutes to evaluate an applicant's portfolio. Forcing them to navigate a 3D avatar across a world or drive a vehicle through 4 gates creates excessive cognitive load and friction.

The **Recruiter Fast Track ("VIEW ENGINEERING DEMONSTRATION")** solves this:
- **Instant 1-Click Access**: Located prominently in the top HUD menu (`[DEMO]` button) and spawn kiosk.
- **Auto-Loads Deterministic Benchmark**: Instantly populates authoritative telemetry without requiring driving.
- **6-Step Interactive Carousel**: Steps through the full software engineering stack in under 120 seconds.
- **Skippable & Non-Locking**: The visitor can pause, scrub, advance, or exit to the 3D world at any moment.
