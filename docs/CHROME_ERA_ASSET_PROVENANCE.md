# Chrome Era asset provenance

The campus is an independent ASISH engineering portfolio. No official McLaren, Hamilton, Mercedes or Formula 1 affiliation is claimed.

New visual work is procedural: pixel textures and metal profiles, original ASISH sign plates/insignia, garage fit-out, sculpture trim, track furniture, geometric social card and uniform styling. Existing voxel car/avatars/buildings are refined in place. No historical photo, sponsor artwork, team logo or driver likeness was downloaded or added.

Historical reading (palette/period context, not redistributed artwork):
- [McLaren MP4-23](https://www.mclaren.com/racing/heritage/formula-1/cars/2008-formula-1-mclaren-mp4-23/)
- [McLaren Hamilton heritage](https://www.mclaren.com/racing/heritage/formula-1/drivers/lewis-hamilton/)
- [McLaren 2008 retrospective](https://www.mclaren.com/racing/heritage/how-good-were-mclaren-lewis-hamilton-2008-formula-1/)
- [Formula 1 year-by-year](https://www.formula1.com/en/information/mclaren-year-by-year.6Gj22qyOorq5dpniarY3rP)

The playable/static car is labeled a **modernised Chrome Era simulator**, with a protective frame and functional digital display. It is not an exact 2008 model. No period-specific replica exhibit is claimed; era-specific grooved tyres and open-cockpit replica geometry were not added.

Three.js RoomEnvironment/PMREM code is used through the existing Three.js dependency and its license. Existing Google Fonts have fallback system fonts. Existing avatar/profile raster provenance and repository licensing still require owner confirmation. The unused legacy team-branded garage photo is preserved locally and excluded from Vercel CLI uploads; it is not used by the game. No source-code reuse license is invented.
