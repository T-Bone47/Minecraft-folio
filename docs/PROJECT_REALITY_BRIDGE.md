## 2. Mathematical Formulations across Portals

### 2.1 RaceMind AI — Tyre Degradation & Pit Delta
The instantaneous tyre degradation is modeled as:
$$\Delta t_{\text{deg}}(l) = \alpha \cdot l^{\beta} + \gamma \cdot \max(0, T_{\text{surface}} - T_{\text{opt}})^2$$
Where:
- $l$: Stint lap age.
- $\alpha$: Compound base wear factor (Soft: $0.030$, Medium: $0.020$, Hard: $0.014$).
- $\beta$: Wear curvature exponent ($1.15 \le \beta \le 1.50$).
- Pit Loss Delta: $22.0\text{s}$ under green flag vs $12.5\text{s}$ under full safety car.

$$\Delta t(s) = \int_{0}^{s} \left( \frac{1}{v_A(\sigma)} - \frac{1}{v_B(\sigma)} \right) d\sigma$$
Eliminating the temporal desynchronization that invalidates time-domain corner comparisons.
